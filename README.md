# DINOSTRAT
The database of regional organic-walled dinoflagellate cyst events. This is DINOSTRAT version 2.2, with data predating January 2026.

Updates: Now, every 'active' and 'questionable' dinocyst species (according to the Fensome et al., 2026 citation index that will come out later in 2026) has at least one entry of a first and last occurrence in DINOSTRAT. If nothing else, such species will have a first and last occurrence around the indicated age of the type section of that species, in the place of the type section. Although this is in many cases an incomplete and unprecise report of their total occurrence range, it is the best guess age of that species for until more detailed stratigraphic information is available. All these 'best guess' ranges are put in as "tier 5" entries, and are indicated to not be accurately tied. Other changes include the addition of more papers that tie dinocyst taxa to the international time scale, from modern surface sediments as well as palaeorecords. Taxonomy and synonymy was updated to changes predating January 2026. 

Please cite the version 1.0 paper Bijl, 2022 Earth System Science Data (https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-14-579-2022) when using this database, as per the CC-BY 4.0 license.

DINOSTRAT version 2.2 consists of 3 data frames:

1. Paleolatitude.csv This dataframe hosts the modern geographic position and paleolatitudinal evolution of sedimentary sections or outcrops used in this paper. The paleolatitude paths of all sites were updated with the recent release of paleolatitude.org version 3.0.
Explanation of columns: 
source (chr): the citation of the paper that presents dinocyst events from this location; 
location (chr): unique name for each geographic location;
tier (chr): the tier of calibration. For further information, see Bijl, Earth System Science Data, 2022; 
geography (chr): geographic region, or country of the study;  
latitude (dbl): present-day latitude of that location (in degrees); 
longitude (dbl): present-day longitude of that location (in degrees); 
age (dbl): the age of the paleolatitude of that location (in Ma); 
min_age (dbl): age of the top of the section (in Ma);
max_age (dbl): age of the base of the section (in Ma);
reference frame (character): all "vaes", for Vaes et al., 2023);
paleolatitude (dbl): the paleotatitude of that location and age (in degrees); 
min_plat (dbl): the lower uncertainty limit of that paleolatitude (in degrees);
max_plat (dbl): the upper uncertainty limit of that paleolatitude (in degrees);
plate name (chr): the name of the tectonic plate of the "location" (output from paleolatitude.org);
version (dbl): the version of DINOSTRAT that the entry was added;


2. modernst.csv This dataframe hosts the surface sediment sample locations. Source for this data is Marret et al., 2020, references therein, Mertens et al., 2014, and Thöle et al., 2023. The modified datasets from those sources are used here.
Explanation of columns: 
source (chr): source of the surface sediment location; 
location (chr): a unique name for each surface sediment station; 
latitude (dbl): present-day latitude of that station; 
longitude (dbl): present-day longitude of that station; 

3. Dinoevents_v2.2.csv This data frame hosts the regional dinoflagellate cyst events, their calibration to the GTS2020, their age and paleolatitude.
Explanation of columns: 
place (chr): a contraction of "location" and "geography" (see below);
location (chr): Location of the event. Corresponds to location column in Paleolatitude.csv; 
Family (chr): The suprageneric classification based on Fensome et al., (1993) and later changes; 
taxa (chr): a contraction of "genus", "species" and "subspecies" (see below); 
genus (chr): The taxonomic genus of the species based on Fensome et al., (1993) and Fensome et al., (submitted); 
species (chr): The taxonomic species name based on Fensome et al., (1993) and Fensome et al., (submitted); 
subspecies (chr): The taxonomic subspecies name of based on Fensome et al., (1993) and Fensome et al., (submitted), [NA] if no subspecies exists. Autonyms fall back on their respective species parent; 
original entry (chr): the calibration of that event as recorded from the "source"; 
tied (lgl): whether the age of the event was tied to the Geologic time scale (TRUE) or based on the age of the type section (FALSE);
source (chr): study that presented the event; 
geography (chr): geographic region, or country of the study;
tier (chr): the tier of calibration. For further information, see Bijl, Earth System Science Data, 2022;
FO_LO (chr): [FO] if the event is a first stratigraphic occurrence, [LO] if the event is a last stratigraphic occurrence; 
calibration (chr): calibration of the event to the Geologic Time Scale 2020 (Gradstein et al., 2020); 
age (dbl): Age of the event, calculated through its "calibration"; 
pal_lat (dbl): paleolatitude of the event, interpolated from the paleolatitude of the site in "Paleolatitude.csv", using its "age";
latitude (dbl): present-day latitude of that location; 
longitude (dbl): present-day longitude of that location;
minage (dbl): the minimum age of that "taxa";
maxage (dbl): the maximum age of that "taxa".

4. modernsp.csv This dataframe hosts the dinocyst composition of the surface sediment sites modernst.csv. We adapted the data from Marret et al., 2020, Thöle et al., 2023, Mertens et al., 2014 and Kim et al., 2025. Suprageneric classification was added, Relative abundance data was converted to presence of taxa. 
Explanation of columns:
location (chr): Location name of the event; 
Family (chr): The suprageneric classification based on Fensome et al., (1993) and later changes; 
taxa (chr): a contraction of "genus", "species" and "subspecies" (see below); 
genus (chr): The taxonomic genus of the species based on Fensome et al., (1993) and Fensome et al., (submitted), with exceptions (see text in Bijl, 2022); 
species (chr): The taxonomic species name based on Fensome et al., (1993) and Fensome et al., (submitted); 
subspecies (chr): The taxonomic subspecies name of based on Fensome et al., (1993) and Fensome et al., (submitted), [NA] if no subspecies exists. Autonyms fall back on their respective species parent; 
tied (lgl): whether the age of the event was tied to the Geologic time scale (TRUE) or based on the age of the type section (FALSE);
source (chr): study that presented the event; 
tier (chr): the tier of calibration. For further information, see Bijl, Earth System Science Data, 2022;
FO_LO (chr): [FO] if the event is a first stratigraphic occurrence, [LO] if the event is a last stratigraphic occurrence. As these are modern, they are by definition all [LO]; ; 
calibration (chr): These are calibrated to 0 Ma by default in this review, so "surface sediment" given;
age (dbl): Age of the event, in this case calculates to 0 Ma by default; 
pal_lat (dbl): paleolatitude of the event, equals modern latitude of the site.
latitude (dbl): present-day latitude of that location; 
longitude (dbl): present-day longitude of that location;
minage (dbl): the minimum age of that "taxa". for the modern species all 0 Ma by default;
maxage (dbl): the maximum age of that "taxa".
